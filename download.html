Street Bites

A restaurant game by Abir's. Build 14.

▶ Play in the browser

Works on a computer, phone or tablet. Always the newest version.

Play now

To keep it on your computer and play offline: open the game in Chrome or Edge, then click the install button at the right end of the address bar (a small screen with an arrow), or the ⋮ menu, then "Install".

🪟 Windows (offline)

Download for Windows
  1. Open the downloaded file.
  2. If Windows says "Windows protected your PC", click More info, then Run anyway.
  3. It installs by itself and puts Street Bites on your desktop and in the Start menu.
  4. F11 switches full screen on and off.

🤖 Android (offline)

Download the APK
  1. Open the downloaded file and allow installing from this source if asked.
  2. To update later, install the new file over the old one. Do not uninstall first, or your saves are lost.

The browser, the Windows app and the Android app each keep their own saved games.
